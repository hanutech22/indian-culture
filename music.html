Music & Instruments

India has the world's richest musical tradition — over 3000 years of pure melody! 🎼

🎸 Famous Indian Instruments

Click an instrument card to discover its sound description and fun facts!

🥁

Tabla

A pair of drums played with fingers and palms. Makes sounds like "dha dhin na tun." The heartbeat of Indian classical music! Played sitting cross-legged.

Percussion
🪕

Sitar

A beautiful string instrument with a long neck and round gourd body. The legendary Ravi Shankar made it famous worldwide. Has 18–20 strings — very complex!

String
🎸

Veena

The oldest Indian instrument — 5000+ years old! Played by Goddess Saraswati herself. A beautiful plucked string instrument from South India, sacred and divine.

Sacred String
🪈

Bansuri (Flute)

Lord Krishna's beloved instrument! A simple bamboo flute that creates the most beautiful, soul-touching melodies. The sound of the flute represents peace and devotion.

Wind
🥁

Mridangam

A double-headed drum from South India. Used in Bharatanatyam and Carnatic classical music. Made from jackwood with animal skin. Has a very rich, resonant sound!

Percussion
📯

Shehnai

A wind instrument with a haunting, beautiful sound. Played at weddings, temples, and celebrations. Ustad Bismillah Khan made it internationally famous with his soul-stirring music!

Wind
