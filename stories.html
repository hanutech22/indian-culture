The Lion and the Mouse

Panchatantra • Moral: Kindness is always repaid
😴🦁

Once upon a time in a great forest, a big, mighty Lion was sleeping under a tree. He was the King of the Jungle. Everyone was afraid of him...

🐭!

A tiny little mouse was running and accidentally ran right over the Lion's nose! The Lion woke up ROARING and caught the mouse in his giant paw!

🙏🐭

The little Mouse trembled and said, "Please, O great King! Set me free! Someday, I promise I will help you!" The Lion laughed but let the mouse go.

🕸️🦁

One day, hunters came and caught the Lion in a big net! The Lion roared and struggled, but could not escape. He was trapped!

✂️🐭❤️

The little Mouse heard the Lion's cries and rushed over. Quickly, she gnawed through the ropes of the net with her sharp teeth and freed the Lion!

🌟🤝

Moral of the Story:

"A small act of kindness can lead to a BIG reward. Never underestimate anyone — even the smallest friend can be your greatest helper!"

Tortoise & the Hare

A slow tortoise and a speedy hare have a race. The hare takes a nap thinking he will win easily — but the tortoise keeps going steadily and wins! Moral: Slow and steady wins the race!

🦅🐍

The Crane & the Snake

A wise crane helps fish in a pond using clever thinking. It shows that intelligence and quick thinking can solve problems that strength cannot. Moral: Use your brain cleverly!

🐘🐁

Elephant's Pride (Jataka Tale)

A proud elephant would not help smaller animals. One day he got stuck in mud — and only the small animals could help him! Moral: Never be proud. Help others and they will help you!
