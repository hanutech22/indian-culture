Indian Food Magic

India has over 1000 dishes! Let's explore the amazing flavors from every corner of India! 😋

🗺️ Taste the Regions of India

🥘

🌏 North India

Dal Makhani, Butter Naan, Rajma, Paneer Butter Masala, Biryani. North Indian food is rich and full of spices like cumin and coriander. Roti (flatbread) is eaten every day!

🥥

🌏 South India

Idli, Dosa, Sambhar, Rasam, Coconut Chutney, Appam. South Indian food uses lots of coconut and rice. Idli is steamed — very healthy! Dosa is crispy and delicious!

🥗

🌏 West India

Dhokla, Thepla, Poha, Vada Pav, Pav Bhaji, Shrikhand. Gujarat loves sweet food while Maharashtra loves spicy! Dhokla is steamed and very fluffy!

🐟

🌏 East India

Macher Jhol (fish curry), Rasgulla, Sandesh, Aloo Posto. Bengal is famous for fish and sweets! Rasgulla is a famous white sweet that melts in the mouth!

🌶️

🌏 Northeast India

Rice, Bamboo Shoots, Fermented foods, Pork dishes. Northeast cuisine is very unique and uses many herbs and plants from the jungle. Very healthy and natural!

🍬

🌏 Festival Sweets

Ladoo, Kheer, Halwa, Modak, Jalebi, Barfi. Every festival has its special sweet! Modak is Lord Ganesha's favorite! Ladoo is made for every happy occasion!
