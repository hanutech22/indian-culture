🎨 Classroom Activity Ideas

Creative, hands-on activities that bring Indian culture alive in your classroom.

🪔

Clay Diya Making

Use clay or salt dough to make diyas. Let children decorate with colors. Teach the story of Diwali during the activity.

CraftDiwaliAll Ages
🎨

Rangoli with Chalk

Use colored chalk on black paper to create Rangoli patterns. Start with dots, then connect. Develops fine motor skills.

ArtMotor Skills5+ Years
🌱

Plant Tulsi Together

Bring a Tulsi sapling to class. Teach children about this sacred plant's medicinal properties and its role in Indian homes.

NatureAyurveda
🥁

Rhythm Circle

Clap simple tabla rhythms: "dha dhin dhin dha". Children echo. Develops rhythm, listening skills and music appreciation.

MusicAll Ages
🗺️

India's Food Map

Draw a simple India map. Children place food stickers (idli-South, paratha-North, dhokla-West, macher jhol-East) on regions.

FoodGeography6+ Years
🗣️

Namaste Around India

Teach "Hello" in 6 Indian languages: Namaste, Vanakkam, Namaskara, Namaskar, Sat Sri Akal, Adaab. Practice together!
