🌍 India's Gift to the World: Numbers!

The numbers 0–9 that the WHOLE WORLD uses today were invented in India! Arab traders learned them and called them "Arabic numerals" — but they came from ancient Indian mathematicians! Zero (शून्य, Shunya) was invented by Aryabhata in India around 500 AD!
