Yoga is India's gift to the world! 5000 years old and still the best exercise! 🌍

🌟 About Yoga

Yoga was born in India over 5000 years ago! The word "Yoga" comes from Sanskrit meaning "to join" — joining body and mind. The United Nations declared June 21 as International Yoga Day because of India! Every year, crores of people around the world practice yoga. It keeps us healthy, calm, and happy! 🌸

🧘 Kids Yoga Asanas (Poses)

These poses are specially gentle for children aged 3-8. Try them every morning!

🌲

Vrikshasana (Tree Pose)

Stand tall like a tree! Stand on one leg, place the other foot on your knee. Raise your arms up like branches. Hold for 10 seconds. Benefits: balance, focus, strong legs!

⛰️

Tadasana (Mountain Pose)

Stand straight and tall like a mountain! Feet together, arms at your sides, chin up. Take 5 deep breaths. Benefits: posture, confidence, breathing!

🦋

Baddha Konasana (Butterfly)

Sit on the floor, bring the soles of your feet together. Flap your knees up and down like butterfly wings! Benefits: hip flexibility, calming energy!

🐍

Bhujangasana (Cobra Pose)

Lie on your tummy, place palms near shoulders, gently lift your head and chest up like a cobra raising its hood. Benefits: strong back, opens chest!

🌙

Balasana (Child's Pose)

Kneel down, sit back on heels, stretch arms forward and rest head on the floor. This is the resting pose. Benefits: calming, relieves stress, rests the mind!

☀️

Surya Namaskar (Sun Salutation)

12 poses flowing together like a greeting to the Sun! A complete workout that exercises every part of the body. Do it every morning! Benefits: full body health!

💨 Pranayama — Breathing Exercises

Pranayama means controlling your breath. It is the secret superpower of Yoga!

🎈 Balloon Breathing Exercise

🌬️

Click Start to begin!

Breathe IN when the circle grows. Breathe OUT when it shrinks. Do this 5 times!

👃

Anulom Vilom

Alternate nostril breathing. Brings calm and improves concentration. Great before exams!

🐝

Bhramari (Bee Breath)

Make a humming sound like a bee! Closes eyes, covers ears gently. Relieves stress and anxiety.

🌬️

Deep Belly Breathing

Breathe deep into the belly, let it rise and fall. 4 counts in, hold 4, out 4. Very calming!
