Festivals of India

India celebrates 100s of festivals every year! Let's learn about the most joyful ones! 🌺

🪔 Diwali 🎨 Holi 💃 Navratri 🌙 Eid 🌾 Pongal

🌟 Our Beautiful Festivals

🪔

Diwali — Festival of Lights

Diwali is celebrated when Lord Ram came home after 14 years! People light lamps (diyas), burst crackers, eat sweets, and wear new clothes. It shows that light always wins over darkness!

🗓️ Month: October/November  |  🌏 All over India

🎨

Holi — Festival of Colors

Holi is the happiest festival! We throw colored powder and water on each other. It celebrates the victory of devotee Prahlad over evil. Colors represent love, joy, and friendship!

🗓️ Month: March  |  🌏 North & West India

💃

Navratri — Nine Nights of Dance

Navratri means "nine nights." We worship Goddess Durga for 9 days! In Gujarat, people dance Garba all night. Each day is a different color. Garba is one of the world's biggest dance events!

🗓️ Month: Sep/Oct  |  🌏 Gujarat & all India

🌙

Eid — Festival of Gratitude

After one month of fasting (Ramadan), Muslims celebrate Eid with prayers, delicious foods like biryani and sewaiyan, and gifts. Eid teaches us to share with those who have less.

🗓️ Month: Varies  |  🌏 All over India

🌾

Pongal — Harvest Festival

Pongal is Tamil Nadu's biggest festival. Farmers thank the Sun God and the sacred cow for a good harvest. "Pongal" means overflow — the sweet rice dish flowing over the pot is a sign of abundance and happiness!

🗓️ Month: January  |  🌏 Tamil Nadu

🌾

Baisakhi — Punjabi New Year

Baisakhi is the Punjabi harvest festival on 14th April. Farmers dance Bhangra and Gidda to celebrate. It also marks the founding of the Sikh Khalsa. The Bhangra dance is now famous worldwide!

🗓️ Month: April 14  |  🌏 Punjab & Haryana
